# NextPlay Solutions — Brand Kit

## The idea

The NextPlay mark is called **Aperture**: a forward play arrow created by removing space from a solid square. The visual idea mirrors the business—find the opening hidden inside an operation, then make the NextPlay clear.

## Brand lines

- **Brand story line:** “Find the opening. Make the NextPlay clear.”
- **Customer promise:** “Your unfair AI advantage”

Use the brand story line in company introductions, presentations, proposals, and longer-form messaging. Use the shorter customer promise in campaign headlines, social graphics, website calls to action, training covers, and branded merchandise. Avoid placing both lines beside the same logo unless the layout has enough space for a clear hierarchy.

## Primary logo

Use `svg/nextplay-primary-horizontal.svg` on white, paper, or other light backgrounds. Use `svg/nextplay-primary-horizontal-inverse.svg` on Forest or other dark backgrounds. The SVG files scale cleanly for websites, documents, presentations, signage, and print.

Use `svg/nextplay-stacked.svg` when the available space is closer to square. Use the mark-only files for avatars, social profiles, app icons, embroidery, hats, and other small-format uses.

Use `svg/nextplay-primary-horizontal-tagline.svg` or its inverse counterpart when the customer promise should travel with the logo.

## Color system

| Color | Hex | Recommended use |
|---|---:|---|
| Forest | `#06120F` | Primary brand color, dark backgrounds, text |
| Deep Pine | `#10231C` | Panels, supporting dark surfaces |
| Signal Lime | `#C9FF3D` | Primary accent, calls to action, signal moments |
| Signal Mint | `#84FFD0` | Secondary accent and metadata |
| Warm Paper | `#F4F4ED` | Documents and light editorial backgrounds |
| Graphite | `#17231E` | Body copy on light backgrounds |
| White | `#FFFFFF` | Inverse copy and clean space |

For standard four-color print, have the print vendor convert these source colors to the correct CMYK profile for its press and material. Do not rely on a generic CMYK conversion for critical color matching.

## Typography

- **Primary:** Geist or Inter
- **Fallback:** Helvetica Neue, Arial, sans-serif
- **Technical labels:** Geist Mono, SF Mono, Menlo, monospace
- Headlines: medium weight, tight tracking, sentence case
- Body copy: regular weight, open line spacing
- Labels: uppercase mono, widely tracked

The SVG lockups retain editable text for flexibility. For final mass-production artwork, ask the vendor or designer to convert wordmark text to outlines. The mark-only SVG files already contain paths only.

## Clear space and minimum size

Keep clear space around the logo equal to at least half the width of the square mark. Do not place type, trim lines, or other logos inside this area.

- Horizontal logo: minimum 120 px digital or 1.25 in print
- Mark only: minimum 24 px digital or 0.35 in print
- For embroidery under 0.6 in, use the mark only and request a sew-out proof

## Correct usage

- Forest mark and wordmark on Warm Paper or white
- Signal Lime mark and white wordmark on Forest
- Solid black monochrome logo when production permits only one ink
- Mark only for social avatars, favicons, hats, and small swag
- Signal pattern as a supporting background, never as a replacement for the logo

## Avoid

- Do not stretch, skew, rotate, bevel, or add drop shadows to the logo
- Do not recolor the logo outside the supplied palette
- Do not fill the negative-space play arrow
- Do not place the primary logo on a busy photo without a solid field behind it
- Do not use Signal Lime for long paragraphs of text
- Do not separate “Next” and “Play” onto different lines in the horizontal lockup
